# Hush — Private Conversations

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A private room can be opened with no account and no saved inbox; what matters is the conversation, not a profile.
- **Identity:** Hush's deep pine, phosphor green, warm paper, and restrained ember. Fraunces gives the brand a human editorial voice; Inter carries the crisp product details.
- **Hero:** The official Hush app icon, a living room-key glyph, and a spare chat interface built from the real product's language.
- **Motion:** Signal-dot match cuts, typography that compresses into a room key, and a single continuous aperture that opens from one person to a conversation.
- **Arc:** An intimate opening pulse widens into a room, moves through the mechanics of joining and leaving, then resolves to a clear brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The ending empties the room into one small green signal; its pulse is the same first-frame pulse, closing the loop without a hard reset.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.80s / 4.80s — “A LITTLE SPACE.”

**Purpose:** Make privacy feel like a quiet human pause rather than a security alarm.

**Exact content:** At 0.12s, tiny mono eyebrow “HUSH / PRIVATE ROOMS”. At 0.42s, Fraunces headline “A little / space.” At 1.62s, supporting line “For the things you mean to say.” At 2.48s replace it with “No audience. Just your people.” Hold until the outgoing move.

**Composition:** Background #0D1713. Small phosphor dot begins at x=73%, y=47%, 1.2vmin. Four thin concentric rings are centered there; only one animates at a time. Type owns the left 56%, 12vmin Fraunces with the 4–6% safe inset. Paper text, green dot, single ember micro-rule. Finish with the ring at 70% frame width, surrounding the same dot.

**Artistic idea:** Silence is visualized as a measurable pocket of negative space; the signal is tiny until the room makes room for it.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00 dot is already visible; its pulse travels once through the concentric rings. 0.12 eyebrow rises 8px and resolves. 0.42 “A little / space.” sharpens from 10px blur as a narrow paper rule traces left-to-right. 1.62 supporting sentence is revealed through a clipped mask. 2.48 supporting sentence replaces the first in a hard typographic beat. From 3.95, rings expand toward camera and the headline shears 2° toward the dot; at 4.80 the ring crosses the full frame.

**Transition contract:** The live dot remains fixed at (73%, 47%). From 3.95s the widening ring crosses the shot boundary at 4.80s and becomes Shot 02's key's circular eye. Two mechanics overlap: radial scale and masked background fold.

**Assets and technique:** No generated imagery. SVG concentric rings, CSS mask, Fraunces and Inter from Google Fonts. Hush icon path supplied at `public/images/hush-icon.png` for Shot 05.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental ambient electronica; opening pulse at 0.00s and scene handoff at the 4.80s downbeat. No voiceover or effects requested.

### Shot 03 / 9.60–14.40s / 4.80s — “A ROOM, NOT AN INBOX.”

**Purpose:** Make a temporary conversation feel present and useful while avoiding claims that Hush can control recipients' copies.

**Composition:** Deep pine. A real Hush-style console takes 65% frame width, slightly right of center, dark card with thin border. A narrow terminal header runs across top; message bubble one in phosphor green, second in paper-tinted dark card. Left column carries the headline only after message exchange. Warm amber timestamp/online marker. Console depth from one restrained shadow plane, not glow.

**Artistic idea:** The physical room is temporary, but the exchange appears before the explanation; the viewer sees a conversation form, then sees its local-memory boundary.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00 horizontal key rule arrives as terminal top rail and the status marker becomes a live point. 0.20 header snaps into focus. 0.55 green bubble scales from the rail with no lateral slide. 1.15 second bubble enters from a 12° perspective tilt and settles. 1.75 left copy “Messages live in memory.” masks on. 2.85 the bottom memory trace shortens and its label brightens, transferring attention without implying messages disappear before the user leaves. 3.45 the recipient caveat appears beneath, calm and legible. At 4.50 the entire console pushes forward, then the next shot opens on the same axis as a split privacy field.

**Transition contract:** The console's outgoing push overlaps the next shot's clipped two-color panel unfold; the keyline and split boundary share the same horizontal axis.

**Assets and technique:** All artwork is a noninteractive illustration, not a functional mock. SVG signal/keyhole and message geometry; actual copy matches Hush's privacy notes. No account names or false security claims.
